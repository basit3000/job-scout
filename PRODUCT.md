# Muhammad Basit Zaheer — Portfolio

## Register
brand

## Platform
web

## Users
Recruiters, classmates, and fellow players who want a quick, memorable read on who Basit is — student, builder, and Dota 2 enthusiast.

## Purpose
A personal map of Basit’s life online: projects, writing, certifications, games, and books — with a Dota-inspired Radiant/Dire identity that feels intentional, not costume.

## Positioning
Not a generic developer Linktree. A competitive, personal “atlas” themed around Radiant daylight and Dire night, with minimap navigation and light easter eggs.

## Brand personality
Competitive, curious, warm under the HUD — sharp type, faction color, map metaphors. Voice words: **competitive**, **personal**, **atlas**.

## Anti-references
- Cream / parchment / “warm SaaS” portfolios
- Purple gradients, Inter-everywhere, glassmorphism-as-default
- Nested cards, gradient text, bounce easing
- Generic typing-hero + identical social cards with no brand POV

## Accessibility
Prefer WCAG AA contrast (≥4.5:1 body). Theme toggle labeled Radiant/Dire. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keep touch targets ≥44px where interactive.

## Conversion & proof
Primary actions: reach out (email / LinkedIn / GitHub), scan projects, read blogs. Proof: real projects, live Spotify/Steam status, certifications, visit counter.
